CBSE Curriculum

Academic Excellence at Every Level

At Ridhima World School, we follow the curriculum prescribed by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), which is designed to provide a balanced and holistic education. Our curriculum emphasizes conceptual understanding, critical thinking, and the application of knowledge in real-world contexts.

We supplement the CBSE curriculum with innovative teaching methodologies, project-based learning, and technology integration to make learning engaging and meaningful for every student.

Classes & Programs

Education tailored to every stage of a child's development

Classes I–V

Primary School

Subjects: English, Hindi, Mathematics, Environmental Studies (EVS), Art, Music, Physical Education, Computer Science.

Focus: Activity-based learning, foundational literacy and numeracy, curiosity-driven exploration, and social skills development.

Classes VI–VIII

Middle School

Subjects: English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Mathematics, Science, Social Science, Computer Science, Art, Physical Education, Music.

Focus: Subject-specific depth, critical thinking, laboratory work, project-based learning, and study skills.

Classes IX–X

Secondary School

Subjects: English, Hindi/Sanskrit, Mathematics, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Social Science, Computer Applications, Art, Physical Education.

Focus: CBSE Board examination preparation, concept clarity, application-based learning, and career awareness.

Classes XI–XII

Senior Secondary

Streams: Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce (with/without Maths), Humanities (Arts).

Focus: Specialized subject study, competitive exam preparation (JEE/NEET/CUET), career guidance, and research skills.

Teaching Methodology

Modern pedagogy that puts students at the centre of learning

Experiential Learning

Learning by doing — students engage in hands-on activities, experiments, and real-world projects that make concepts come alive.

Collaborative Learning

Group projects, peer discussions, and team activities that develop communication, teamwork, and leadership skills.

Technology-Enabled

Smart classrooms, digital resources, and educational technology that enhance the learning experience and digital literacy.

Differentiated Instruction

Teaching tailored to individual learning styles and paces, ensuring every student receives the support they need.

Inquiry-Based

Encouraging curiosity and questioning — students learn to ask, investigate, and discover answers through structured inquiry.

Value-Based

Integrating values and life skills into every lesson, helping students develop strong character alongside academic knowledge.

Assessment & Evaluation

A fair and comprehensive system that measures true learning

Continuous Assessment (CA)

Regular formative assessments including quizzes, class tests, oral assessments, projects, and assignments throughout the academic year. This helps teachers identify learning gaps early and provide timely support.

Term Examinations

Two term-end examinations (Half-Yearly and Annual) that comprehensively assess student understanding of the curriculum covered during each term.

Co-Scholastic Assessment

Evaluation of co-curricular activities including art, music, sports, work experience, and life skills, ensuring holistic development is measured and valued.

Board Examinations

For Classes X and XII, students appear for CBSE Board examinations. Rigorous preparation includes pre-board exams, mock tests, and remedial classes.
