Academic Programs

Comprehensive education from foundation to senior secondary

Classes I–V

Primary School

Activity-based learning that builds strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, and curiosity about the world. Focus on holistic development through play, exploration, and creativity.

Classes VI–VIII

Middle School

Developing critical thinking, subject depth, and study skills through the CBSE middle-school curriculum. Emphasis on conceptual clarity and application-based learning.

Classes IX–X

Secondary School

Rigorous preparation for CBSE Board examinations with focus on conceptual clarity and application. Regular assessments, remedial classes, and personalised guidance.

Classes XI–XII

Senior Secondary

Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams with career guidance and competitive exam preparation. Expert faculty and well-equipped laboratories for practical learning.

  • Periodic tests and term exams
  • Project and practical work

Teaching Methodology

How we make learning engaging and effective

Experiential Learning

Hands-on activities, experiments, and real-world projects that make abstract concepts concrete and memorable.

Collaborative Projects

Group work and peer learning that develop teamwork, communication, and leadership skills.

Technology-Enabled

Smart classrooms with digital tools that enhance understanding and engagement.

Personalised Attention

Small class sizes and individual mentoring ensure every student gets the support they need.
