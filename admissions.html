Admission Process

A simple and transparent process to join our school community

Step 1: Enquiry

Fill out the enquiry form online or visit the school office.

Step 2: Application

Submit the completed application form with required documents.

Step 3: Interaction

Interaction/assessment with the student and parents.

Step 4: Confirmation

Admission confirmation and fee payment.

Eligibility Criteria

Age requirements as on 31st March of the admission year

Class I

Minimum age: 6 years. Birth certificate required.

Class II–V

Age appropriate to the class level. Previous school's report card required.

Class VI–VIII

Age appropriate. Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous school mandatory.

Class IX

TC and report card from previous school. Subject to assessment.

Class XI

Class X pass with minimum required percentage for stream selection.

Class XII

Admission only in exceptional cases with valid reasons and CBSE approval.

Required Documents

Please prepare the following documents for admission

For All Classes

  • Birth Certificate (original + photocopy)
  • Aadhaar Card of student
  • Aadhaar Card of parents
  • Passport-size photographs (4 copies)
  • Previous school's Report Card / Mark Sheet
  • Transfer Certificate (for Class II onwards)
  • Character Certificate (for Class IX onwards)
  • Migration Certificate (for Class XI, if from other board)

For Class XI (Additional)

  • Class X Board Examination Mark Sheet
  • Class X Passing Certificate
  • Original Transfer Certificate (duly countersigned)
  • Migration Certificate (if from non-CBSE board)
  • Stream selection form (available at school office)
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate (if applying for concession)

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common admission queries

What is the medium of instruction? +

English is the primary medium of instruction. Hindi is taught as a compulsory subject, and Sanskrit is offered as a third language in middle school.

Is the school co-educational? +

Yes, Ridhima World School is a co-educational institution with separate facilities for boys and girls where required.

Does the school provide transport? +

Yes, the school provides safe and reliable bus transport with GPS tracking covering Mainpuri city and nearby areas. Transport fees are separate from tuition fees.

Are there any scholarships available? +

Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships for deserving students. Sibling concessions are also available. Please contact the school office for details.

What is the student-teacher ratio? +

We maintain a healthy student-teacher ratio of approximately 25:1 to ensure personalized attention for every student.

Does the school have a library and labs? +

Yes, we have a well-stocked library and fully-equipped Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer Science laboratories.

Can I schedule a campus visit? +

Absolutely! We encourage prospective parents and students to visit our campus. Please contact the school office to schedule a visit.
