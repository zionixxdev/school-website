Admission Process

Simple steps to join our school

1

Enquiry

Submit an online enquiry or visit our campus to learn about availability and programs.

2

Application

Fill out the admission form and submit required documents along with the registration fee.

3

Interaction

Attend a friendly interaction/assessment session with the student and parents.

4

Confirmation

Receive admission confirmation and complete fee payment to secure your seat.

Required Documents

Documents for Admission

  • ✓
    Birth Certificate: Original and photocopy from municipal authority.
  • ✓
    Transfer Certificate: From previous school (for Class II onwards).
  • ✓
    Report Card: Previous year's mark sheet / report card.
  • ✓
    Passport Photos: 4 recent passport-size photographs of the student.
  • ✓
    Aadhaar Card: Photocopy of student's and parents' Aadhaar cards.
  • ✓
    Address Proof: Current residential address proof.
  • ✓
    Medical Certificate: Fitness and immunisation record.
