Facilities & Student Life

Modern infrastructure and vibrant student life that supports holistic development

Home / Facilities

World-Class Infrastructure

Modern facilities designed to support every aspect of student development

Smart Classrooms

Spacious, well-lit classrooms equipped with interactive smart boards, projectors, and digital learning tools that make lessons engaging and interactive.

Physics Laboratory

Fully-equipped physics lab with modern instruments for mechanics, optics, electricity, and electronics experiments.

Chemistry Laboratory

Modern chemistry lab with fume hoods, safety equipment, and chemicals for qualitative and quantitative analysis.

Biology Laboratory

Well-stocked biology lab with microscopes, specimens, models, and slides for detailed biological study.

Computer Laboratory

Modern computer lab with 40+ systems, high-speed internet, and latest educational software for digital literacy.

Library

Extensive library with 5,000+ books, reference materials, periodicals, and digital resources for research and reading.

Sports Ground

Large playground with cricket pitch, basketball court, volleyball court, and athletics track for outdoor sports.

Indoor Sports

Dedicated indoor sports area for table tennis, chess, carrom, and badminton for year-round physical activity.

Auditorium

500-seat auditorium with professional stage, sound system, and lighting for assemblies, performances, and events.

Music Room

Dedicated music room with instruments including harmonium, tabla, keyboard, and guitar for music education.

Art Room

Creative art studio with easels, art supplies, and display space for painting, drawing, and craft activities.

Transport

Fleet of GPS-enabled buses with trained drivers and attendants covering Mainpuri and surrounding areas.

Medical Room

On-campus medical room staffed with a trained nurse, equipped with first-aid and basic medical facilities.

Cafeteria

Clean and hygienic cafeteria serving nutritious and balanced meals prepared in a certified kitchen.

Security & CCTV

24/7 security with CCTV surveillance across campus, trained guards, and controlled entry for student safety.

Student Life

Vibrant experiences beyond the classroom

Clubs & Societies

Science Club, Eco Club, Debate Society, Drama Club, Robotics Club, Literary Club, and more for students to explore their interests.

Sports

Cricket, basketball, volleyball, athletics, yoga, table tennis, chess, and more with inter-house and inter-school competitions.

Cultural Activities

Music, dance, drama, art, and craft activities culminating in annual cultural fest and celebrations.

Competitions

Inter-house and inter-school competitions in academics, sports, arts, and cultural activities throughout the year.

School Houses

Four houses — Surya, Chandra, Vayu, and Agni — fostering teamwork, leadership, and healthy competition.

Student Council

Elected student council with Head Boy, Head Girl, and house captains developing leadership and responsibility.

Educational Trips

Field trips, excursions, and educational tours that bring classroom learning to the real world.

Community Service

Community service initiatives and social awareness programs that develop empathy and social responsibility.
