Welcome to Ridhima World School

A School Where Every Child Thrives

Founded in 2022, Ridhima World School is a CBSE-affiliated co-educational institution in Mainpuri, Uttar Pradesh. We believe that every child has unique talents waiting to be discovered. Our mission is to provide a stimulating learning environment that combines academic rigour with character development, preparing students to become confident, compassionate, and responsible global citizens.

With modern infrastructure, a dedicated faculty, and a curriculum that balances tradition with innovation, we are committed to nurturing the leaders of tomorrow.
