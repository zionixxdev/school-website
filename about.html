Our Story

A Legacy of Learning Since 2022

Founded in 2022, Ridhima World School is a CBSE-affiliated co-educational institution in Mainpuri, Uttar Pradesh. What began as a vision to bring world-class education to the region has grown into a thriving community of learners, educators, and parents committed to excellence.

We believe that every child has unique talents waiting to be discovered. Our mission is to provide a stimulating learning environment that combines academic rigour with character development, preparing students to become confident, compassionate, and responsible global citizens.

With modern infrastructure, a dedicated faculty, and a curriculum that balances tradition with innovation, we are committed to nurturing the leaders of tomorrow.

Our Milestones

The journey so far

2022

Foundation & Inauguration

Ridhima World School was established with a vision to bring quality CBSE education to Mainpuri, opening its doors to the first batch of students.

2023

CBSE Affiliation

Achieved Central Board of Secondary Education affiliation, validating our commitment to national academic standards.

2024

Infrastructure Expansion

Added state-of-the-art science laboratories, a modern computer lab, and expanded sports facilities to enrich student learning.

2025

Academic Excellence

Our first batch of students achieved outstanding board results, with multiple students securing distinctions and national-level competition medals.

2026

Growing Community

Now serving over 500 students with a dedicated faculty of 35+ educators, we continue to expand our programs and facilities.
