About Our School

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

Founded in 2022, Ridhima World School is a CBSE-affiliated co-educational institution located in Mainpuri, Uttar Pradesh. Born from a vision to provide world-class education in the region, our school has quickly established itself as a centre of academic excellence and holistic development.

At Ridhima World School, we believe that education extends far beyond textbooks. Our approach combines the rigour of the CBSE curriculum with innovative teaching methodologies, co-curricular activities, and a strong focus on values and character development. We strive to create an environment where every child feels safe, valued, and inspired to achieve their personal best.

With modern infrastructure, a dedicated and experienced faculty, and a student-centric philosophy, we are committed to nurturing confident, compassionate, and capable individuals who are ready to face the challenges of a rapidly changing world.

Vision & Mission

Guiding principles that shape everything we do

Our Vision

To be a premier centre of educational excellence that empowers students to become lifelong learners, critical thinkers, and responsible global citizens who contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Mission

To provide a holistic and inclusive education that nurtures intellectual curiosity, fosters creativity, builds character, and prepares students to meet the challenges of the 21st century with confidence and competence.

Our Commitment

To create a safe, stimulating, and supportive learning environment where every child is valued, every voice is heard, and every talent is nurtured towards its full potential.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the story of Ridhima World School

2022

Foundation

Ridhima World School was established with a vision to bring quality education to Mainpuri. The school opened its doors with initial enrolment of 150 students across primary and middle grades.

2022

CBSE Affiliation

Received affiliation from the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), marking a significant milestone in our commitment to academic standards.

2023

Infrastructure Expansion

Added state-of-the-art science laboratories, computer lab, and a well-stocked library. Introduced smart classroom technology across all sections.

2023

First Annual Day

Celebrated our first Annual Day function with enthusiastic participation from students, parents, and the community. The event showcased the diverse talents of our students.

2024

Senior Secondary Section

Expanded to include Classes XI and XII with Science and Commerce streams, providing students with broader academic opportunities.

2025

First CBSE Board Results

Our first batch of Class X students achieved a 100% pass rate in the CBSE Board examinations, with several students scoring above 90%.

2026

Growing Stronger

With over 500 students, 35+ faculty members, and a growing list of achievements, Ridhima World School continues to expand its horizons and impact in the region.
